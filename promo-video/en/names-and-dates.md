Type these into the cards. Spelling is **identical** in English, Spanish, and Portuguese (no accents). The family matches a **~68-year-old woman** in the US / LatAm — the actual viewer, not a young nuclear family.

Ages below are as of **18 August 2026**.

## Why these names

The main cut is English, but Mexico, Colombia, and Brazil must **read the names at a glance**. Rule: same letters in EN / ES / PT, common in **both** North America and Latin America.

### Locked (use)

| Name | Gender | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| **Laura** | F | Most universal of the set. Normal in the US, Mexico, Colombia, Brazil. |
| **Daniel** | M | Same. The default male name for this ad. |
| **Clara** | F | Short, no accent, readable everywhere. |
| **Lucas** | M | Very common in Brazil; clear in ES/EN. |

**Backup names** (if a name is already on a leftover card, or a retake needs a double):

- Men: **David**, **Samuel**, **Gabriel**
- Women: **Victoria**, **Amanda**, **Diana**, **Sofia** (write `Sofia`, never `Sofía`)

### Do not use in the EN/LatAm cut

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| José, María, Sofía, João, Juan, Nicolás, Sebastián | Accents or language-specific spelling |
| Ana vs Anna, Sara vs Sarah | Split EN vs ES/PT |
| Liam, Noah, Olivia-as-English-hit | US/Canada only |
| Andrea | Male in some countries, female in others |
| 1958-08-07 | Guest seed on the site |

## Why these dates

- **Laura, 1958** sits in the 65+ majority without using the seed day (we use **12 March**, not 7 August).
- **Daniel, 1955** is 2–3 years older — typical for this generation.
- **Clara, 1984:** mother was **26**. Plausible in the US and in Mexico / Colombia / Brazil (LatAm first births often a little earlier; 26 still works).
- **Lucas, 1987:** ~3-year gap, ordinary spacing.
- Birthdays sit in **March / June / September / November**, so on a given day the **year and month hands differ** between cards. The family of clocks should not look like three copies of one dial.
