# Audience — why the ad looks this way

Figures below are **views, last 365 days** (channel analytics used to lock the scenario). They are the reason for the names, ages, languages, and “papa / mama / children” dates — not decoration.

## Regions

| Region | Share | What it means for the ad |
|--------|------:|--------------------------|
| United States | 39.6% | Lead market. English UI. Names must look normal in the US. |
| Mexico | 7.4% | Spanish-speaking; same Latin names, no English-only names (Liam, Noah). |
| Colombia | 4.3% | Same as Mexico. |
| Brazil | 4.1% | Portuguese; names must match **without** accents (`Sofia` not `Sofía` / `João`). |
| Canada | 2.7% | Same English cut as the US. |

North + Latin America together are the frame. One picture, one family, three overlay languages (EN / ES / PT). Do not shoot a “US-only” family or a “LatAm-only” family.

## Age and gender

| Slice | Share |
|--------|------:|
| Women | 62.6% |
| Men | 37.4% |
| 13–17 | 0.7% |
| 18–24 | 0% |
| 25–34 | 4.2% |
| 35–44 | 8.6% |
| 45–54 | 12.0% |
| 55–64 | 22.7% |
| 65+ | **51.8%** |

**Core viewer:** a woman about **60–75**, mostly in the US, also Mexico / Colombia / Brazil.

Consequences:

1. **First card is the mother (Laura).** She is the viewer. Do not open on a child.
2. **“Children” means adults ~38–42**, not kindergarten. That is who a 68-year-old mother actually has. A toddler on screen would miss the audience.
3. A **grandchild** would be emotionally true for 65+, but the brief was papa / mama / children, and 13–17 is almost absent as *viewers*. Skip the grandchild unless a later cut needs a third generation.
4. Do not cast a 30-year-old “starter family.” That is 4% of views.

## Languages (captions / channel)

| Language | Share |
|----------|------:|
| No captions | 88.1% |
| English | 8.5% |
| Spanish | 0.9% |
| English (US) | 0.6% |
| Portuguese | 0.4% |

Most people watch **without** captions. Overlay must work as **large type on the picture**, not as burned-in subtitle paragraphs.

- **Main cut:** English overlay + English UI. Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking viewers still read **Laura / Daniel / Clara**.
- **Extra cuts:** same picture, swap overlay to Spanish or Portuguese (cheap).
- **Russian:** its own shoot (or a dedicated overlay pass). Not mixed into the EN/LatAm picture.
