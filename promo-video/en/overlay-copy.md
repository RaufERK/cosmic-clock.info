# Overlay copy

Site everywhere: **cosmic-clock.info**

Keep each card to **3–6 words**. Channel views are mostly **without captions**; the type *is* the soundtrack for people who watch muted.

Prefer **see / your cycles** over a hard “control the situation.” On English it sounds corporate; on this product the honest claim is: in about a minute you **see** the cycles (yours and the family’s). A milder “stay ahead / toma el control” line is optional, not the default.

## English — main cut

**Open (large):**  
`cosmic-clock.info`

**Slogan (minimum):**  
`Easy. In a minute — your cycles.`

**Slightly longer:**  
`Easy and fast.`  
`See your cycles — and theirs.`

**Close:**  
`cosmic-clock.info`  
`See your cycles — and theirs.`

**Optional, stronger:**  
`See your cycles. Stay ahead.`

**Two more slogans:**  
`Plan your future.`  
`Be ready for change.`

## How to use on the timeline

1. First card of type: **URL + minimum slogan**.
2. While filling forms: **no slogan** (or a small URL only). Let the typing and the clock read.
3. Last hold: **URL + “and theirs / близких / quienes amas.”**

Do not put the long and the minimum slogan on screen at the same time.
