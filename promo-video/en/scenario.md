# Scenario

Short screen-recording ad. The viewer watches someone **use the real site** on a phone-sized frame. Between the actions: large type with the URL and one line about speed + cycles.

Shoot date assumed for ages: **18 August 2026**.

## Runtime

Aim **20–35 seconds** for the main cut. Three cards (Laura, Daniel, Clara). If it still feels calm, add Lucas.

## Picture (same for EN / ES / PT)

1. **Open on the phone frame** with the site already loaded (guest or empty list — not the admin, not login). URL is not the hero yet; the first overlay will be.
2. **Overlay A** (large): `cosmic-clock.info`  
   Under it, one line (see [overlay-copy.md](overlay-copy.md)). Hold ~2 seconds.
3. **Add clock.** Type **Laura**, date **12 March 1958**. Do not rush the name: it must be readable. Submit / next until the **form becomes the clock**. Hold on the finished dial ~2 seconds (hands must be visible).
4. **Add clock.** **Daniel**, **4 November 1955**. Same: name readable, then the transformation. Two clocks now sit as a couple.
5. **Add clock.** **Clara**, **20 June 1984**. Third transformation = “and your loved ones / their cycles.”
6. Optional: **Lucas**, **8 September 1987**.
7. **Hold the family of clocks** on screen.
8. **Overlay B:** URL again + closing line (`See your cycles — and theirs.` / locale equivalent).
9. End on the URL. No login, no password, no settings.

## What must be visible

- The **name** being typed (cast is the localization strategy).
- The **date** being chosen (month name + day + year).
- The **form → clock** motion. That is the product demo. Cut away too early and the ad has no proof.
- After two or three cards: **different hand positions**. Dates were chosen so the dials do not clone each other.

## What stays off camera

- Guest seed card **The Summit Lighthouse / 1958-08-07** (delete it before recording, or start from a clean guest state after the seed was cleared).
- Auth, errors, reorder/lock mode, admin.
- DevTools code panel, laptop menu bar, desktop wallpaper. Record **only** the phone viewport (see [recording-setup.md](recording-setup.md)).

## Overlay placement

- Top or center, **large**. One idea per card.
- Do not cover the clock face during the transformation.
- During typing, overlay can dip or sit in a thin top bar; during the hold on finished clocks, overlay can be larger again.
