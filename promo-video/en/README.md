## One-line brief

Record the real site on a **phone-sized window**: type a name and a date, watch the **form become a clock**, then add a second and third family card. Superimpose the URL and a short line: easy, about a minute, you see **your cycles and theirs**.

## Locked choices

- **Site:** `cosmic-clock.info`
- **Format:** laptop screen recording, Chrome device toolbar, **iPhone 14 Pro** (390 × 844). Not Pro Max. Not Samsung.
- **UI language for the EN / LatAm cut:** English.
- **Names in the form:** Latin spelling that is identical in English, Spanish, and Portuguese (**Laura**, **Daniel**, **Clara**, optional **Lucas**).
- **Family:** mother + father + adult daughter (optional adult son). Not toddlers. Not the guest seed date `1958-08-07`.
- **Russian:** a **separate** short cut, same actions; swap overlay copy (and names only if you want).
- **Overlay length:** 3–6 words per card. People cannot read a paragraph over a screen recording.

## What this ad is selling

Cosmic Clock v1: a **life-event card** with a start date. From that date the dial shows year / month / day **stage** (Prophet Cosmic Clock). Not natal astrology, not planets.

The visual hook is the **form → clock** transformation. The emotional hook for this audience is **family cycles** (spouse and grown children), not a teenage self-clock.
